# FixIt – Home Services & Emergency Roadside Assistance (RSA) Marketplace

FixIt is a full-stack, on-demand home repair and emergency service platform built with **Flutter (Material 3)**, **Node.js / Express**, **MongoDB Atlas**, **Razorpay Payments**, and **Firebase**.

## 🌟 Highlights
- **19 Trade Categories & 85+ Services**: Electricians, Plumbers, On-Road RSA Breakdown, General Mechanics, AC & HVAC, Locksmiths, Cleaning, and more.
- **⚡ Blinkit-Style Instant Quick Commerce Dispatch**: Rapid 15–30 minute emergency dispatch with roadside GPS pinning.
- **Dynamic Platform Fee & Transparent Pricing**: Dynamic ₹25 platform fee; verified technicians keep 100% of their labor quote.
- **💳 Razorpay Payment Gateway & Refund Engine**: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, VPA), Cards, Net Banking, Cash after service, coupons (`FIXIT50`, `WAIVEFEE`), and instant cancellation refunds.
- **🛠️ Technician Partner App & Admin Console**: Real-time job broadcast radar, 4-stage lifecycle stepper, KYC verification, and live analytics.
